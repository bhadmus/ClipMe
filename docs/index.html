About ClipMe

Straight from the README.

ClipMe is a clipboard extension app for macOS. It lives in your menu bar and remembers your clipboard history, lets you organize reusable snippets, and gives you quick keyboard shortcuts to paste anything you have copied.

ClipMe is an independent, open-source fork of Clipy (which is itself based on ClipMenu), distributed under the MIT License.

Universal build. ClipMe ships as a Universal 2 binary and runs natively on both Apple silicon (arm64, M1 or later) and Intel (x86_64) Macs, so no Rosetta translation is required on Apple chips while Intel Macs remain fully supported.

Build from source

Development environment: macOS 26 Tahoe · Xcode 26.5

  1. Open ClipMe.xcodeproj in Xcode.
  2. Switch to ad-hoc signing: open Configurations/CodeSigning.xcconfig and uncomment #include "Configurations/CodeSigning-AdHoc.xcconfig".
  3. Build and run the ClipMe scheme.

Firebase is optional — add your own GoogleService-Info.plist in ClipMe/GoogleService, or skip it for local builds. See the repository for full instructions and the privacy policy.
